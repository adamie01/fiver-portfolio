How I work

Three steps, no surprises

You always see the site before it is final.

1

You send the basics

Name, address, phone or WhatsApp, opening hours, your menu or services and a few photos. A phone picture of the menu is fine.

2

I send a preview

You open it on your own phone and tell me what feels wrong. Colours, order, wording: this is the moment to change things.

3

You get the files

I deliver the finished site and walk you through putting it online with your own domain name.
